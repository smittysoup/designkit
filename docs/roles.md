# People and authority

Business owners establish real work, exceptions, authority and the evidence behind
baseline outcomes. Product owns the bounded transformation scope and measures of
value. Design owns understandable human-agent interaction, intervention and
experience review. The forward-deployed engineer connects direct stakeholder
learning to executable prototypes, tool boundaries, models and evaluations.
Engineering leads assess invariants, durable state, runtime boundaries and
implementation feasibility. Relevant domain/security specialists contribute where
the actual process requires their judgment.

An authoring assistant proposes, drafts, checks and executes authorized reversible
work. It cannot invent stakeholder decisions. The business agent being designed
owns a separate coherent outcome and explicit limits. Keep the distinction in
onboarding, prototypes and reviews.

People can hold several roles; no committee, enterprise RACI or hosted approval
system is required. Record who accepted which scope and revision, and link actual
evidence. A synthetic example has fictional actors, not real approvals. When
constraints change accepted authority or business meaning, record an owned decision
and revisit affected experience/model/contracts/evaluations. Routine technical
refinements can proceed inside the agreed boundary.
