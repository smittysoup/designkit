# Applying DesignKit

Transform business processes into AI-native agent operating models. Select an initiative explicitly and resume from its manifest, decisions and questions. Understand current work and baseline before assigning agents. Eliminate, combine and simplify work; retain deterministic tasks and human judgment where appropriate. You are an authoring assistant, not the business agent.

Keep the BRD canonical. Preserve IDs and source evidence. Specify outcome, tools, knowledge, state, completion, authority, intervention and recovery for every business agent. Never invent human acceptance, performance claims or live execution. Keep material unknowns owned and blocking where necessary.

Use seven iterative capability guides. Demonstrate the human-agent loop and exceptions, then derive data-model.md, diagrams and contracts. Compare downstream changes explicitly. These suggested instructions supplement, never replace, existing project instructions.
