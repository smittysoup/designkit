# DesignKit validate

This capability helps transform a business process into an AI-native agent operating model.

## Select and resume
Select `--root` and `--initiative` explicitly. Read manifest.json, decisions.md, open-questions.md and constitution.md under design/<initiative>/, then only the relevant canonical artifacts. Apply the consuming repository's instructions. You are an authoring assistant; the business agents being designed have separate authority.

## Inputs
Prototype, baseline, evaluation expectations and source revision. Optional: existing prototype or stakeholder notes. When evidence is missing, record the gap and continue reversible work; ask for material business decisions only.

## Work
Exercise success, ambiguity, intervention, denied authority, retries and failure cases. Record observation, expected outcome and evidence level. Ask identified humans to judge usefulness and authority; never invent their acceptance. State cost, latency and reliability obligations for later real-agent tests.

## Owned outputs
validation/, evaluation-plan.md, open-questions.md. Resolve transformation filenames under transformation/. Use templates under the installed package assets as guides. The BRD owns FR/NFR meaning; indexes reference it. Preserve authored decisions, IDs, provenance and unrelated content. Mark affected evidence and scoped reviews stale after source or authority changes; do not refresh evidence hashes without rerunning.

## Completion
Observed results, failures and owned production verification obligations. File presence is not acceptance. Separate draft/review state, executed evidence and scoped readiness. Finish with links to changed artifacts, what ran versus what was simulated, unresolved owners and the next capability that reduces uncertainty. Capabilities are iterative, not a waterfall.
