# DesignKit envision

This capability helps transform a business process into an AI-native agent operating model.

## Select and resume
Select `--root` and `--initiative` explicitly. Read manifest.json, decisions.md, open-questions.md and constitution.md under design/<initiative>/, then only the relevant canonical artifacts. Apply the consuming repository's instructions. You are an authoring assistant; the business agents being designed have separate authority.

## Inputs
Current work, outcome, authority and constraints. Optional: existing prototype or stakeholder notes. When evidence is missing, record the gap and continue reversible work; ask for material business decisions only.

## Work
For each current step choose eliminate, combine, simplify, deterministic, agent or human, with reasons. Assign coherent outcomes to business agents, not one agent per step. Define knowledge, tools, durable state, completion and stopping conditions. Identify human decisions, intervention, waits, escalation and recovery. If agency adds no value, record that result.

## Owned outputs
agentic-process.md, agent-catalog.md, evaluation-plan.md. Resolve transformation filenames under transformation/. Use templates under the installed package assets as guides. The BRD owns FR/NFR meaning; indexes reference it. Preserve authored decisions, IDs, provenance and unrelated content. Mark affected evidence and scoped reviews stale after source or authority changes; do not refresh evidence hashes without rerunning.

## Completion
A defensible change in work allocation and explicit limits. File presence is not acceptance. Separate draft/review state, executed evidence and scoped readiness. Finish with links to changed artifacts, what ran versus what was simulated, unresolved owners and the next capability that reduces uncertainty. Capabilities are iterative, not a waterfall.
