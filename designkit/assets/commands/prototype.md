# DesignKit prototype

This capability helps transform a business process into an AI-native agent operating model.

## Select and resume
Select `--root` and `--initiative` explicitly. Read manifest.json, decisions.md, open-questions.md and constitution.md under design/<initiative>/, then only the relevant canonical artifacts. Apply the consuming repository's instructions. You are an authoring assistant; the business agents being designed have separate authority.

## Inputs
Future process, catalog, selected scenario and constraints. Optional: existing prototype or stakeholder notes. When evidence is missing, record the gap and continue reversible work; ask for material business decisions only.

## Work
Implement a narrow runnable human-agent loop: trigger, question, authorized answer, resumed work and reviewable outcome. Keep the human final decision distinct. Show an exception and recovery. Use synthetic data and visible simulation labels. Capture actual desktop/mobile states with source hashes; do not use generated illustrations as evidence.

## Owned outputs
experience/, architecture/state-transitions.md. Resolve transformation filenames under transformation/. Use templates under the installed package assets as guides. The BRD owns FR/NFR meaning; indexes reference it. Preserve authored decisions, IDs, provenance and unrelated content. Mark affected evidence and scoped reviews stale after source or authority changes; do not refresh evidence hashes without rerunning.

## Completion
The selected loop and exception execute with reproducible evidence. File presence is not acceptance. Separate draft/review state, executed evidence and scoped readiness. Finish with links to changed artifacts, what ran versus what was simulated, unresolved owners and the next capability that reduces uncertainty. Capabilities are iterative, not a waterfall.
