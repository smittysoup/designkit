# DesignKit discover

This capability helps transform a business process into an AI-native agent operating model.

## Select and resume
Select `--root` and `--initiative` explicitly. Read manifest.json, decisions.md, open-questions.md and constitution.md under design/<initiative>/, then only the relevant canonical artifacts. Apply the consuming repository's instructions. You are an authoring assistant; the business agents being designed have separate authority.

## Inputs
Representative business cases, exceptions, decision owners and existing observations. Optional: existing prototype or stakeholder notes. When evidence is missing, record the gap and continue reversible work; ask for material business decisions only.

## Work
Interview the business owner about trigger, inputs, handoffs, decisions, rework and outcomes. Record measured baselines with sources; explicitly mark unknowns. Separate deterministic work from judgment. Identify one bounded outcome before proposing agents.

## Owned outputs
current-process.md, brd.md. Resolve transformation filenames under transformation/. Use templates under the installed package assets as guides. The BRD owns FR/NFR meaning; indexes reference it. Preserve authored decisions, IDs, provenance and unrelated content. Mark affected evidence and scoped reviews stale after source or authority changes; do not refresh evidence hashes without rerunning.

## Completion
A recognizable process, a named outcome owner, and owned uncertainty. File presence is not acceptance. Separate draft/review state, executed evidence and scoped readiness. Finish with links to changed artifacts, what ran versus what was simulated, unresolved owners and the next capability that reduces uncertainty. Capabilities are iterative, not a waterfall.
