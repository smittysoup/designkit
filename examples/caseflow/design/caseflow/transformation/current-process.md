# Current manual work

Synthetic CaseFlow reference. Authored design hypothesis; no stakeholder acceptance or production performance claim.

## Baseline

No measured baseline exists for this fictional process. A future pilot would observe elapsed time, touch time, clarification count and rework across representative cases; product owns sampling and the business owner validates representativeness. OUT-1 is a hypothesis, not a quantified benefit.

## Actors

Requester supplies workshop facts; specialist gathers evidence and writes a package; approver judges the package. Manual messages and copied notes fragment context. Business authority remains with the approver.

## Work

CUR-1: Requester emails intake; specialist asks for missing participant count and recopies the answer. CUR-2: Specialist gathers evidence, drafts a package and coordinates review. CUR-3: Approver inspects and records a decision; requester changes often trigger a new email chain.

## Exceptions

Missing facts cause waits. Evidence tool outages delay preparation. A changed requirement can make an earlier review stale. See current-process diagram for handoffs; real frequency and impact must be observed before a pilot.
